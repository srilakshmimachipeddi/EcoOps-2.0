# Campus Energy Policy (Version 4.2)
**Institution**: Green Valley University Facilities & Sustainability Directorate  
**Effective Date**: January 2024  
**Classification**: Standard Operating Policy  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Objectives
Green Valley University is committed to achieving net-zero emissions by 2035. All building management systems (BMS), heating ventilation air conditioning (HVAC) controllers, lighting arrays, and smart power meters are governed under this policy. Automated energy optimization software must align with peak shaving, occupant health standards, and thermal comfort criteria.

## 2. Baseline Consumption & Monitoring
Smart sub-meters record electrical consumption (kWh), reactive power (kVAR), and peak demand (kW) at 15-minute intervals. 
- Academic buildings have standard baseline operating profiles between 07:00 and 20:00 on weekdays.
- Out-of-hours baseline (21:00 to 06:00) must not exceed 18% of peak daytime load.
- Any out-of-hours consumption deviation exceeding +25% over rolling baseline requires immediate automated investigation.

## 3. HVAC Schedule & Setpoint Governance
- **Cooling Season Setpoints**: Standard indoor temperature setpoint is 24.5°C ± 1.0°C.
- **Pre-Cooling Protocol**: Pre-cooling of lecture theatres, auditoriums, and administrative wings is permitted between 04:00 and 06:30 using low-tariff grid energy.
- **Off-Peak Load Shifting (Section 4.2)**: 
  - To minimize peak commercial tariff exposure between 14:00 and 18:00, automated chillers and variable refrigerant flow (VRF) units are authorized to reduce output by up to 20% by shifting cooling load to off-peak night cycles (22:00 to 06:00).
  - Routine summer seasonal surges (+10% to +25%) during heatwaves (>35°C ambient) with normal telemetry should trigger automated scheduling optimization rather than manual dispatch.

## 4. Anomaly Thresholds & Safety Bounds
- **Automated Interventions**: Permitted only when diagnostic confidence exceeds 75% and sensor calibration is verified within 90 days.
- **Prohibited Actions**: Automated systems are strictly forbidden from modulating electrical breakers, transformers, or fire safety pressurization fans during anomalous electrical spikes.
