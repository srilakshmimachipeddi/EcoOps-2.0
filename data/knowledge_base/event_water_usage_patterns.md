# Event Water Usage Patterns & Campus Surge Protocols (2022 Archive)
**Source**: Campus Event Management and Auxiliary Services  
**Category**: Operational Precedents

---

## 1. Student Celebrations, Sports Meets & Annual Festivals
Campus-wide gatherings, cultural fests, and Annual Day events trigger significant transient demand surges across designated residential and sports zones:
- **Hostel Zones**: Annual Day celebrations historically account for a +10% to +15% surge in domestic water draw due to guest accommodations, stage preparation, and event cleaning.
- **Duration**: Transient surges typically endure for 24 to 36 hours before returning to standard baselines.

## 2. Decision Logic for Mixed Event & Sensor Signals
When an anomaly of +30% to +40% coincides with a scheduled campus festival AND a recent sensor servicing event:
- The festival explains roughly +10% to +15% of the increase.
- The remaining +20% to +25% could be a legitimate sub-surface leak OR sensor miscalibration.
- **Protocol**: System confidence is MEDIUM (0.55 - 0.65). Notify the facilities desk with a non-emergency advisory, suggest sensor calibration verification, and continue monitoring before taking physical shutdown steps.
