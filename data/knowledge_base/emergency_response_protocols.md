# Emergency Facilities Response Protocols & Electrical Safety Standard
**Target**: Facilities Engineering, Security Services, Environmental Health & Safety (EHS)  
**Emergency Level**: Code Red / Immediate Action

---

## 1. Catastrophic Energy Spikes & Safety Hazards
Any building experiencing sudden out-of-hours consumption surges > 200% (specifically extreme spikes > 300% during overnight hours 22:00 - 05:00) represents an acute safety threat:
- **Potential Hazards**:
  - Electrical short circuit or transformer dielectric breakdown
  - Lithium battery thermal runaway in research laboratories
  - Chemical fume hood scrubber motor seizure / potential fire hazard
  - Major cooling tower pump cavitation or burst piping
  - Unauthorized building intrusion or severe equipment tampering

## 2. Mandatory Protocol for High/Extreme Outliers
- **AUTONOMOUS OPTIMIZATION PROHIBITION**:
  Automated load-shifting, chiller throttling, or power cycle algorithms are STRICTLY PROHIBITED. Executing load shedding during an electrical fault can trigger arc flash incidents or exacerbate fire conditions.
- **Immediate Escalation Requirements**:
  1. Generate unique incident identifier (`ALERT-XXXX`).
  2. Transmit urgent dispatch dispatch notifications to Facilities Control (`facilities@campus.edu`) and Campus Security Dispatch (`security@campus.edu`).
  3. Place Fire Safety response team on 15-minute standby.
  4. Mandate immediate physical on-site inspection by a certified electrical technician.
