# Campus Water Conservation Guidelines & Leak Management (v3.0)
**Institution**: Campus Sustainability Office & Plumbing Works  
**Focus**: Domestic Water Consumption, Hostels, Dining Commons

---

## 1. Baseline Residential Consumption Metrics
- Hostels and student residences operate on distinct diurnal flow curves:
  - Peak morning demand: 06:00 - 09:30
  - Peak evening demand: 18:00 - 21:30
  - Nighttime minimum flow (01:00 - 04:30): Must not exceed 5% of diurnal peak.
- A sudden unseasonal flow increase (+30% to +50%) during non-peak hours often correlates with underground mainline fissures, leaking overhead gravity cisterns, or flush valve failures.

## 2. Distinction Between Leaks and Operational Fluctuations
- **Real Leaks**: Characterized by persistent elevated baseline during minimum flow window (01:00 - 04:30).
- **Sensor Calibration Artifact**: Common immediately following ultrasonic transducer replacement (indicated by step changes without downstream tank level depletion).
- **Intervention Guidelines**: If sensor replacement was recent (< 72 hours), do not initiate emergency shutdown. Schedule non-disruptive physical inspection and observe for 6-12 hours.
