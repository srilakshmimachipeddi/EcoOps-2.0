# Summer HVAC Optimization Guide (2023 Edition)
**Target Group**: Campus BMS Operators, Sustainability Coordinators, AI Agents  
**Context**: High ambient temperature operational procedures (May - July)

---

## 1. Seasonal Thermal Profile & Anomaly Differentiation
During peak summer months (May, June, July), daily ambient temperatures frequently surpass 38°C to 42°C. 
- A 10% to 20% increase in academic building energy consumption during June is typical and directly correlated with ambient wet-bulb temperature.
- Historical data indicates that +15% energy spikes in Academic Block A, Library, and Science Complex during June are seasonal HVAC load adjustments, not hardware malfunctions.

## 2. Recommended Optimization Interventions
When energy increases are verified to be ambient-driven (+10% to +25% range with operational sensors):
1. **Off-Peak Night Pre-Cooling**: Shift cooling duty cycle to 10:00 PM - 06:00 AM.
2. **Variable Speed Drive (VSD) Modulation**: Throttle air handling unit (AHU) fan speeds to 75% during unoccupied afternoon slots (12:00 PM - 02:00 PM).
3. **Deadband Widening**: Expand temperature deadbands by 1.0°C during peak grid pricing hours.

- **Average Energy Savings**: -1,000 to -1,500 kWh per building monthly.
- **Estimated Cost Savings**: -₹4,000 to -₹6,000 INR per building monthly.
- **Carbon Abatement**: -450 to -650 kg CO₂ equivalent per month based on regional grid emission factors.
